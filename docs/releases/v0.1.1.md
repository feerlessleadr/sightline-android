## Sightline for Android 0.1.1

### What is new

- **Screen reader support (TalkBack).** Camera tiles say their name and what is happening on them (motion, triggered, no
  signal, the AI label), with actions to open the camera or change its tile size. Alerts read as one sentence with Play and
  Flag or protect actions. Filter buttons say whether they are selected. The player's timeline says where you are and has
  steps back and forward (15 seconds, 5 minutes, 1 hour) and Go to live, because dragging is not possible with a screen
  reader. The pop-up, the Activity map and chart, and the colour legend can be read too.
- **Wide screens and tablets.** Settings, Server status, System log, Activity, About and the colour legend keep to a readable,
  centred width. On a screen 840 dp wide or more the Alerts tab shows the list with an **alert preview panel** beside it: a
  slideshow of eight stills from the chosen alert. Tap an alert (or point at it with a mouse) to choose it; tap the picture or
  Open alert to open the player. Settings > Alert preview panel turns it off.
- **Troubleshooting switch.** Settings > Developer > Show group layout shows Blue Iris's group picture as it is in the
  player, with each camera outlined (green shown, red hidden or disabled). Only useful when something looks wrong in a
  group view.

### Known limits

- Dragging a camera tile to reorder it has no screen-reader alternative yet, and the faintest grey text is lighter than the
  usual contrast target.
- The preview panel has no "Smooth" video mode (stills only), and the new screen reader and wide-screen work has had little
  testing on real devices.
- Everything from 0.1.0 still applies: Blue Iris 6 and an HTTPS address are needed, and PTZ and Blue Iris's own push
  notifications are not in the app (Home Assistant links are).

### Install

Download the APK below, or update from an app such as Obtainium pointed at this repository's releases. It is signed with the
same key as 0.1.0, so it installs over it and keeps your sign-in and settings. Check the download against the checksums at the
bottom.
